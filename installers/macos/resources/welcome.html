CyberDeck

This installer places the CyberDeck engine and its bundled plugins under /usr/local/cyberdeck and registers the engine as a per-user launchd background service so your deck stays reachable after the UI is closed.

To remove CyberDeck later, run sudo /usr/local/cyberdeck/../installers/macos/scripts/uninstall.sh (or the bundled uninstall.sh).
